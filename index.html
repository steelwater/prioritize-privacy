Prioritize Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Prioritize is a local-first app for organizing personal items with user-defined priority tags. Prioritize does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Information handling

The information users enter—including priority tags, item names, notes, starred status, and display preferences—is stored only on the user's device. It is used only to provide the app's organization and display features and is not sent to the developer or any external service.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

Prioritize does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, purchases, third-party sign-in, or tracking technology.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until the user clears the app's storage or uninstalls the app. The developer cannot view, recover, or delete this information remotely because the developer never receives it.

Children

Prioritize is intended only for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
